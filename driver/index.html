Workflow stages

The 6 columns shown on the docket board map to dockets.stage 1 → 6.

1 — Order received order created, awaiting delivery
2 — Skip Bin delivery driver signed delivery
3 — Waste Pickup driver signed pickup
4 — Waste Disposal awaiting completion
5 — Completion driver signed disposal
6 — Invoice office marked invoiced

Reset workflow / reopen

Open any docket and use the actions panel at the bottom of the drawer to reset all signatures or reopen a single stage. Both require a reason and are written to the audit log per ISO 14001:2026 clause 6.3.
